Why is a pilot run to measure the seed spread allowed before pre-registering X, and what rule must it follow?	X must be stated as an effect size, which needs \(\sigma\), and \(\sigma\) is cheap to measure. The pilot's seeds must not be reused as evidence in the registered run.	learnml week-10 week-10-day-5
What does <b>lever</b> mean?	The one thing an idea changes: data (what is seen), architecture (what structure is assumed), objective (what is optimized), optimization (how it is trained) or inference (how it is used).	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>falsifiable prediction</b> mean?	A claim with a number that a run can prove wrong: on task T, A beats B by at least X on metric M. No number, no experiment.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>baseline</b> mean?	The method B your idea must beat. A result only means something relative to a baseline, and the baseline you choose decides how much it means.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>strong baseline</b> mean?	The best simple method that already exists for the task, tuned as carefully as your own method. If it wins, your idea is not needed yet.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>same-compute baseline</b> mean?	The baseline given exactly the training time, data and model size your method used. It separates a real idea from simply spending more.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>control condition</b> mean?	A run that differs from your method in exactly one thing. Like a control condition in a user study: it makes a difference attributable to one cause.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>ablation study</b> mean?	A table of runs where you remove or swap one part of your method at a time, keeping everything else fixed, to show which parts carry the gain.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>effect size</b> mean?	How big a difference is compared with the run-to-run spread. A gain of 2 points means little if repeats of the same setup differ by 3.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>variance across seeds</b> mean?	How much the same setup&#x27;s result changes when only the random seed changes: initial weights, data order, dropout. Measure it before comparing two methods.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>pre-registration</b> mean?	Writing the task, metric, baseline, prediction, seeds and stopping rule down before running, so the result cannot quietly reshape the question.	learnml week-10 vocab
What does <b>compute budget</b> mean?	The total training time you allow an experiment, counted in GPU-hours: runs times seeds times minutes per run. Decide it before the first run.	learnml week-10 vocab
