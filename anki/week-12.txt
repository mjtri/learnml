What does <b>sonification</b> mean?	Turning data into sound by a fixed rule. The vOICe rule scans an image column by column: left to right becomes time, height becomes pitch, brightness becomes loudness.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>spectrogram</b> mean?	A picture of a sound: time runs left to right, pitch bottom to top, brightness is how much energy each pitch carries. A sonified image round-trips to one.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>latent</b> mean?	The short vector a model keeps in the middle: what it extracted from an input. Distances between latents are the model&#x27;s opinion about similarity.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>autoencoder</b> mean?	A network trained to copy its input to its output through a narrow middle, so the middle must keep what matters. An encoder squeezes, a decoder rebuilds.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>reconstruction loss</b> mean?	How far an autoencoder&#x27;s rebuilt output is from its input, usually the mean squared pixel difference. Low means the middle kept enough; it says nothing about which shapes stay apart.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>bottleneck</b> mean?	The narrowest point of a network, where an input must fit into a few numbers. Shape it like a real display and the network learns a code for that display.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>quantization</b> mean?	Rounding a continuous number to one of a few allowed levels, like an 8-level tactor intensity. Cheap to send, but the rounding step has zero slope, so gradients stop there.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>straight-through estimator</b> mean?	The trick that trains through rounding: the forward pass uses the rounded value, the backward pass pretends rounding was the identity, so the gradient passes straight through.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>VAE</b> mean?	An autoencoder whose bottleneck is a noisy vector: the encoder outputs a mean and a spread, a sample is decoded, and a penalty keeps codes near a standard bell shape.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>ELBO</b> mean?	The VAE&#x27;s training objective: reconstruction quality minus a penalty for codes that stray from the standard bell shape. Making it as large as possible is the whole training recipe.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>rank correlation</b> mean?	Agreement between two orderings, from minus 1 to 1: replace each value by its position in its own list, then correlate the positions. Ignores scale, keeps order.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>confusion matrix</b> mean?	A table with one row per stimulus shown and one column per answer given, counting each combination. The off-diagonal cells are the confusions a display causes.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>JND</b> mean?	The smallest change in a stimulus a person detects reliably, measured by psychophysics. A table of JNDs over pairs says which stimuli a display can keep apart.	learnml week-12 vocab
What does <b>kill/pivot/scale decision</b> mean?	The rule written before the run for what happens after it: kill if the gap misses X, pivot if the ablation shows the gain came from elsewhere, scale up otherwise.	learnml week-12 vocab
