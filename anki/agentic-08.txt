What can a free limit reset do, and from where?	Restore a 5-hour or weekly limit once, from Settings › Usage on web or desktop; not from Claude Code, and not undone.	learnml agentic-08 agentic-08-day-2

What makes a rule survive the prune?	A source link or a measured number. A rule with neither is scheduled for a measurement or deleted.	learnml agentic-08 agentic-08-day-3

What does <b>usage report</b> mean?	Six numbers written down once a week from the meters: Claude weekly and worst-window percent, top attribution item, any flag, Codex weekly percent, credits spent.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>/insights</b> mean?	A Claude Code command that writes an HTML report on how you work: projects, friction points, suggestions. Reads local sessions only; its analysis tokens count against your plan.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>budget rule</b> mean?	A written trigger and action for one meter: when this number passes this threshold, do that; with the source or measurement that says the action works.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>seat type</b> mean?	Standard or Premium on ChatGPT Business: which allowance a member gets. Premium has more usage and no 5-hour window; only a workspace owner switches it.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>spend limit</b> mean?	A monthly cap on pay-as-you-go credits: set by you in Claude&#x27;s Settings &gt; Usage, or per seat type and per user by a ChatGPT Business owner or admin.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>weekly review</b> mean?	A fifteen-minute ritual at a fixed weekly slot: write the usage report line, check the week&#x27;s logs against the playbook, change one rule, delete one without evidence.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
What does <b>playbook v1</b> mean?	The first pruned playbook: at most 20 rules, each with a source or a measurement, installed as global instructions in both tools.	learnml agentic-08 vocab
