Dark = low loss. The valley floor runs left–right; the walls are 10× steeper.
learning rate
ⓘ Words used here
SGD
Step = − learning rate × gradient. Steep directions get big steps, shallow ones tiny steps.
momentum
Keep a running average of gradients (memory 0.9) and step along that. Noise cancels; a consistent direction builds up to 10×.
Adam
Running averages of the gradient and of its square; each coordinate moves about one learning rate per step, whatever its steepness.
ravine
A loss surface steep across and shallow along. Real loss surfaces are full of them.
