ⓘ Words used here
test set
n items the model got right or wrong. Here they are drawn at random from a model with the true accuracy you set.
bootstrap
Resample the n results with replacement, recompute accuracy, repeat 1000 times. The 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles are the interval.
standard error
The formula shortcut for a proportion: square root of p(1 − p)/n. About two of them either side is the 95% interval.
coverage
How often, over many draws, the interval contains the truth. A 95% interval should manage it about 19 times in 20.
