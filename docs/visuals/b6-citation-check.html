ⓘ Words used here
DOI
A permanent label like 10.1038/221963a0. The prefix names the publisher: 10.1145 ACM, 10.1109 IEEE, 10.1007 Springer, 10.1038 Nature, 10.1016 Elsevier.
arXiv ID
Year and month, a dot, a serial: 1706.03762 is June 2017. A month above 12, or a year after the paper's, cannot be.
fused reference
A real title attached to another paper's real identifier. The lookup resolves, but to the wrong paper: the verdict "differs".
lookup
Asking Crossref for a DOI or arXiv for an ID. Only this proves a paper exists; a passed pattern never does.
