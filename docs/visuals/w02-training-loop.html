ⓘ Words used here
training loop
forward → loss → zero grads → backward → update, repeated. The order is the content.
MSE
Mean squared error: the average of (prediction − target)² over the four points.
zero grads
Reset w.grad and b.grad to 0 before backward. Off, and each backward adds onto the last one.
loss curve
Loss against step. Falling: learning. Zig-zag: steps too big. Turning upward: something is wrong with a stage.
