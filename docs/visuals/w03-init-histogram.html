ⓘ Words used here
initialization scale
The spread σ of the random starting weights. Each unit's sum of 64 inputs has spread about σ·√64 times its input spread.
saturated
A tanh unit with |output| above 0.99: its slope is nearly zero, so almost no gradient passes through it. For ReLU: units that are exactly zero (dead for this input).
normalize
Rescale each unit's outputs across the 128 inputs to mean 0 and spread 1 before the unit, the way BatchNorm does.
