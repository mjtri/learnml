One batch, one matrix: tap a cell, nudge its similarity, watch the loss

Rows are four images, columns their four captions. Dashed cells are the true partners (positives); every other cell is a negative. Cell shading = softmax probability within its row.

ⓘ Words used here
similarity
Cosine similarity between an image vector and a caption vector: 1 same direction, 0 unrelated.
positive / negative pair
A positive pair is an image with its own caption (the diagonal). Every other image–caption combination in the batch is a negative.
temperature τ
Each row is divided by τ before the softmax. Small τ turns small gaps into confident probabilities.
InfoNCE loss
Per row, minus the log of the probability the softmax gives the true partner. Averaged over rows (and, symmetrically, columns).
