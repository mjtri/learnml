ⓘ Words used here
debugging order
A fixed sequence of checks when a number is off, cheapest and likeliest first; stop at the step that moves the number.
gap
Your number minus the claimed one. Its size says where to look first: near chance means labels; a few points means preprocessing; tenths mean numerics.
tolerance band
Two test-set standard errors, or the seed spread, whichever is larger. A gap inside it needs no explanation.
chance level
What guessing scores: 100 divided by the number of classes.
