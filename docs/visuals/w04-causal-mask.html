ⓘ Words used here
√d scaling
Dividing every score by the square root of the vector size, so typical scores stay near 1 as d grows.
causal mask
Scores pointing at later tokens are set to −∞ before the softmax, so their weights become exactly 0 (hatched cells).
softmax
Exponentiate and divide by the total: a row of scores becomes weights ≥ 0 that sum to 1. Big gaps in scores give a hard pick.
the extra dimensions
The first two numbers of each vector are the lesson's; the rest are fixed random ±1, which is what makes raw scores grow like √d.
