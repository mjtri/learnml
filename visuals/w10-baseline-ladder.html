Three rungs your method must clear: trivial, strong (fairly tuned), same compute

Task: balanced shape classification, accuracy in %. Grey rungs move when you starve the baseline of tuning or steps.
rung 1 · trivial (majority class) rung 2 · strong B, as tuned by you rung 2 · strong B, tuned as hard as yours rung 3 · same-compute B your margin over the fair rung 3
ⓘ Words used here
baseline
The method B your idea must beat. A result is only ever a difference from a baseline.
trivial baseline
Always predict the most common class: 100 / K for K balanced classes. If your model sits near it, the pipeline is broken.
strong baseline
The best simple existing method, tuned as carefully as your own: same number of configurations tried.
same-compute baseline
The strong baseline given the same steps, data and model size as your method.
