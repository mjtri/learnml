y = W x: the columns of W are where the unit arrows land

Drag the blue / orange arrow tips (columns of W) or the hollow dot (input x).
W x y₁ = row 1 · x y₂ = row 2 · x area ×
ⓘ Words used here
dot product
Multiply matching numbers and add: row · x = w₁x₁ + w₂x₂.
matmul
Matrix multiply: each output is one row of W dotted with x.
area × (determinant)
How much W scales areas. 0 means the plane was squashed onto a line: information is lost.
