Tap a number to change its size (1–5). “+ axis” adds one on the left.
ⓘ Words used here
shape
The size of a tensor along each axis, e.g. (3, 4) = 3 rows, 4 columns.
axis
One direction you can index along: rows, columns, a stack…
broadcasting
Line shapes up from the right; a size-1 or missing axis is virtually copied to match.
faded cells
Virtual copies. No memory is used; the same numbers are reused.
