ⓘ Words used here
FLOPs
Floating-point operations: multiplies and adds. Training a transformer costs about 6 per parameter per token.
petaflop-day
10¹⁵ FLOPs per second for one day: 8.64 × 10¹⁹ FLOPs. The unit GPT-3's paper uses.
utilisation
The share of a GPU's advertised speed a real training loop achieves; 25–45% is normal.
compute-optimal
The N and D that give the lowest fitted loss for a fixed C; about 20 tokens per parameter.
