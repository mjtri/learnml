multi-head attention
Split each q, k, v vector into h slices; each slice runs its own attention with its own softmax; the outputs are joined end to end.
head size
Numbers per head: the full width divided by h. Here 4 ÷ h. Scores are divided by its square root.
positional encoding
A fixed vector per position added to q, k and v. With it off, the tokens before you are a bag with no order.
causal mask
Hatched cells: a token cannot look at later tokens.
