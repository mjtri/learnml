ⓘ Words used here
loss surface
Each position is one setting of two parameters; darkness shows how low the loss is there.
gradient
The uphill direction and steepness at your current position. We step the opposite way.
learning rate
How far to step for a given steepness. Too big and you overshoot the valley.
diverge
Each step lands higher than the last; the loss explodes.
