ⓘ Words used here
InfoNCE loss
The contrastive loss: how badly each image fails to pick its own sound out of the batch. With 64 in the batch, guessing scores ln 64 = 4.16.
held-out retrieval
On pairs never trained on: the share of images whose own sound is the nearest held-out sound. Chance is 1 in 60 here, about 2%.
latent sd
Spread of the unit-length latent vectors across a batch, averaged over dimensions. Near zero means every input maps to the same point: collapse.
