ⓘ Words used here
literature review pipeline
Search in both tools, merge and dedupe, verify every reference by identifier, summarise only what survived.
union
Every reference either tool found, counted once: A + B − overlap.
verified fraction
Verified ÷ offered, per tool. Here shared references fail at half the rate, since two tools agreeing is weak evidence a paper exists.
lookup
Asking Crossref for a DOI or arXiv for an ID. The record comes back, or nothing does.
