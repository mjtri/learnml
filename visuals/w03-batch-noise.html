ⓘ Words used here
mini-batch
B examples drawn at random from the 1,000. Its gradient is the mean of their per-example gradients.
spread
Typical distance between a batch estimate and the full-data gradient, over many draws (root mean square). Theory says it shrinks like 1/√B.
uphill
An estimate pointing more than 90° away from the true gradient: a step along it would raise the loss.
