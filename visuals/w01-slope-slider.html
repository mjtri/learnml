ⓘ Words used here
derivative / slope
How fast f changes per unit change of x, at this x. The steepness of the orange tangent line.
numerical gradient
The green estimate: nudge x by h, measure the change in f, divide by h.
ReLU
max(0, x): zero for negative inputs, the input itself otherwise. The most common “bend” in neural networks.
