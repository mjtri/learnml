ⓘ Words used here
column vector
Where the matrix sends one unit arrow. The shaded shape is where the unit square lands.
rank
How many independent directions the outputs fill: 2 for the plane, 1 when the columns line up (a line), 0 when both are zero (a point).
σ (singular value)
The strength of one stretch. Counting the σ that are not zero gives the rank; in floating point you must say what counts as zero.
det
The signed area of the parallelogram; zero exactly when the rank drops below 2.
