ⓘ Words used here
hidden state
The vector a model holds for one example at one layer. Here 2-D so you can see it; in a real model it is hundreds of numbers wide.
linear probe
A straight boundary fitted to labelled points on a frozen model's hidden states. Its accuracy on held-back points says how readable the label is at that layer.
held-back
Points the probe never trained on. The only accuracy worth reporting.
shuffle labels
Randomly reassign the labels so text and label are unrelated. A probe that still scores above chance on held-back points is measuring itself, not the features.
