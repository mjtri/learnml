ⓘ Words used here
logits
The raw scores the model outputs, one per character, before they are turned into probabilities.
temperature
Logits are divided by T before softmax. Below 1 sharpens towards the favourite; above 1 flattens towards uniform. Near 0 it is greedy decoding.
top-k
Keep only the k highest logits; the rest get probability 0, and the survivors are renormalised.
draw
One random pick in proportion to the bars. Sampling is one draw per character.
