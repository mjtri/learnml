ⓘ Words used here
training / validation error
Mean squared error on the points used to fit, and on the held-back points. Only the second says how the fit will do on new data.
overfitting
Training error keeps falling while validation error rises: the curve is memorising the noise in the training points.
weight decay
A penalty λ on the size of the coefficients. Big wiggles need big coefficients, so the penalty smooths the curve.
