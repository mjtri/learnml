ⓘ Words used here
parameter count
Total learned weights. Per block about 12d²: four d×d matrices in attention, d×4d and 4d×d in the MLP. Biases and LayerNorms are left out here.
V, T, d, N
Alphabet size, context length, stream width, number of blocks.
checkpoint
The weights saved to disk: 4 bytes each in 32-bit, 2 in 16-bit.
fits for training
Rough rule: training needs about 16 bytes per weight (weights, gradients, two optimizer copies), so 15 GB holds under a billion.
