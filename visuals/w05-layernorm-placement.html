ⓘ Words used here
LayerNorm
For one token's vector: subtract its mean, divide by its standard deviation. Then a learned scale and shift (not shown).
pre-norm
x ← x + f(LN(x)). The norm sits on the branch; the stream stays a plain sum.
post-norm
x ← LN(x + f(x)). The norm is applied to the sum itself after every block, as in the 2017 figure.
cosine
1 when the final stream points exactly like x₀, 0 when unrelated.
