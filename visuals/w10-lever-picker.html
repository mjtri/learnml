ⓘ Words used here
lever
The one thing an idea changes: data, architecture, objective, optimization or inference. The other four stay frozen.
objective
What the training loop minimises: the loss. Swapping pixel loss for a contrastive loss moves this lever.
inference
How a trained model is used at test time, e.g. averaging several passes. Changing it needs no retraining.
falsifiable prediction
"On task T, A beats B by at least X on metric M." One lever gives one such sentence; two levers give two.
