Method A is truly better than B by the gain you set. How often will n seeds show it?

Dots: one experiment's seed scores (A orange, B blue). Bars: observed A − B gap over all experiments so far. Dashed: the true gain. Grey band: gaps the noise alone can produce.

ⓘ Words used here
seed
The number that fixes a run's random draws. Changing only the seed and re-running measures how noisy one number is.
variance across seeds
How far repeats of the same setup land from each other; here the spread σ of one seed's score.
effect size
The true gain divided by σ. Δ = 1 means the two methods differ by one seed's worth of noise.
noise band
Gaps the dice alone produce most of the time: about ±2 · σ · √(2 / n) around zero. A gap inside it proves nothing.
