ⓘ Words used here
weekly cap
The Claude allowance for seven days across all models. Pace is the share of the week that has passed; a bar well ahead of pace hits the cap early.
worst window
The single 5-hour block that came closest to its limit. High with a low weekly bar means the sessions are bunched.
attribution
The /usage breakdown of recent usage by skills, subagents, plugins, MCP servers and loops, from this machine's session history only.
behavior flag
A /usage warning when long context or cache misses account for 10 % or more of recent usage.
