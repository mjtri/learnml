ⓘ Words used here
accuracy
Fraction of the 100 screenshots labelled correctly, positives and negatives alike.
precision / recall
Precision: of the screenshots flagged, how many were right. Recall: of the true positives, how many were caught.
majority class
The rule that always answers the commoner label. Its accuracy is the real chance level when classes are unbalanced.
split by participant
Test on people the model never saw, so it cannot score by recognising a room or a hand.
