ⓘ Words used here
power law
loss = (N₀ ÷ N)^α: each tenfold increase in N multiplies the loss by the same factor, 10^−α.
log–log plot
Both axes in powers of ten. A power law becomes a straight line; the slope is −α.
floor E
Loss the model can never remove (the noise in the text). It bends the line flat at the right.
decade
One factor of ten on a log axis.
