ⓘ Words used here


pre-registration
Writing task, metric, baseline, prediction, ablation, seeds and stopping rule down before the first run, so the result cannot rewrite the question.


compute budget
rows × seeds × minutes per run, in GPU-hours. A free Colab session gives a few T4-hours at most.


effect size
The gain divided by the spread across seeds. n seeds per row can show effects of about 4 / √n and larger.


stopping rule
A fixed step count decided in advance. "Until it looked converged" lets each row stop where it looks best.
