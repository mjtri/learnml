One weight matrix W and its LoRA change B·A: how many numbers train?

W has d_out × d_in numbers (frozen) B is d_out × r, A is r × d_in trainable, r·(d_out + d_in) share of W that trains W is this many times bigger adapter size for this matrix, 32-bit r where B·A stops saving anything
ⓘ Words used here
W
The pretrained weight matrix of one linear layer, d_out rows by d_in columns. Frozen: it gets no gradient.
B, A
The two thin trainable matrices. Their product B·A has the same shape as W but only rank r.
rank r
The width of the thin matrices: how many independent directions the change may use.
crossover
When r·(d_out + d_in) reaches d_out·d_in the adapter is no smaller than W: r = d_out·d_in / (d_out + d_in).
