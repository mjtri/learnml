ⓘ Words used here
usage delta
How far the weekly bar in /usage (or Codex /usage weekly) moved during the task, in percentage points, read before and after.
quality 1–5
Your score after reading the result: 1 unusable, 3 needed real fixes, 5 shipped as is.
A/B week
One week with the tool installed, logging the same kind of task with it and without it; the three numbers decide keep or uninstall.
noise
One task is one sample. A difference under about 15 % of minutes with equal quality could be the task, not the tool; log a second pair before deciding.
