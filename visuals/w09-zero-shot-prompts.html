A toy zero-shot classifier: three prompts are the whole classifier

Edit a prompt and every tile is re-scored. Words the toy knows: cat, dog, car, bird, truck, kitten, puppy, animal, vehicle, pet, photo, picture, sketch, drawing, cartoon; filler words (a, of, the…) count as "this is a sentence".

ⓘ Words used here
zero-shot classification
No labelled examples of the classes: each class is a sentence, embedded by the text side; the image goes to the closest sentence.
prompt template
The sentence a class name is dropped into ("a photo of a …"). In this toy, as in CLIP, a bare word is scored less reliably than a sentence.
how the toy works
Each tile hides a feature list (object, style, and what its bare word also evokes). A prompt is the sum of its words' features; bare words keep more of their stray meanings. Score = cosine similarity × 10, then softmax.
