ⓘ Words used here
tokenizer artefact
An odd model behaviour caused by how the text was cut, not by the model.
pre-tokenizer
The rule that splits text into word-like chunks before BPE; GPT-2 attaches the leading space to the word, so " world" and "world" differ.
byte
A number 0–255. English letters are one byte each, a Korean syllable is three. Dashed chips are lone bytes, not whole characters.
merge
One learned BPE rule gluing two chunks. The toy learned 500 from English only; GPT-2 learned 50,000 from the web.
