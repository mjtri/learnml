ⓘ Words used here
rank correlation
Replace each list by positions (1 = closest pair, 1 = most-confused pair), then correlate the positions. Ignores how big the numbers are, keeps their order. Ties get the average position.
plain correlation
The usual correlation of the raw values. One extreme value can drag it a long way.
distance
One minus the cosine similarity between the two shapes' class-mean latents: 0 means identical direction. Closeness is 1 minus distance.
