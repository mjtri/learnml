Three tokens: edit q, k, v and watch who reads whom

Weights = softmax(q·k ÷ √2), one row per asking token. Tap a row for its arithmetic.
row scores q·k ÷ √2 exp weights (sum) output = Σ weight × v
ⓘ Words used here
query (q)
What this token is looking for. Dotted against every key.
key (k)
What a token advertises. A query that matches it gets a big score.
value (v)
What a token hands over. The output is the weighted mix of values.
softmax
Exponentiate each score and divide by the total, so a row becomes weights ≥ 0 that sum to 1.
