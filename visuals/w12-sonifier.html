ⓘ Words used here
sonification
Turning data into sound by a fixed rule. Here: each column is a 60 ms snippet, each row a pitch, brightness its loudness. Left to right becomes time.
spectrogram
A picture of the sound: 16 pitch bands (top = high) by 16 time slices; brightness is the energy found in that band during that slice.
round-trip correlation
How closely the spectrogram matches the image you drew, as a correlation from 0 to 1. Blur between neighbouring pitches keeps it below 1.
