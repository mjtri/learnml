ⓘ Words used here


reproduction report
One paragraph, six sentences: target, setup, result, verdict, explanation, residue; committed next to the config and the seed.


tolerance
Two test-set standard errors, or the seed spread, whichever is larger; the band that turns a gap into a verdict.


residue
The part of the gap no measured change accounts for, stated with the next check and its cost.


measured move
How much the number changed when one thing was changed and the run repeated; a guess is not a move.
