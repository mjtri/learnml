Kill, pivot or scale up: the rule reads the numbers, not you

KILL if the gap is under the plan's kill line, the interval includes zero, or the gap never reaches X. The lever is dead; the plan's alternative is the next plan.
PIVOT if the gap survived the kill line with the interval above zero, but the ablation cost less than the plan's floor. The gain is real but not for the claimed reason; start from the plan's pivot target.
SCALE UP if the gap clears X, the interval is above zero, and the ablation cost at least the floor. 50 shapes, real listeners.


ⓘ Words used here


gap A − B
Mean of the metric for your method minus the mean for the baseline, over seeds.


95% interval
The bootstrap range for the gap. If it includes zero, the data cannot tell the gap from no difference.


ablation cost
How much the metric drops when the claimed ingredient (the sound side) is removed. Under the plan's floor means the ingredient did nothing; negative means removing it helped.


kill / pivot / scale up
The three pre-registered outcomes, with the plan's own numbers (X, kill line, ablation floor). "Run more seeds and look again" is deliberately not one of them.
