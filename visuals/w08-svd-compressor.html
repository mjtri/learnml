ⓘ Words used here
SVD
Writes the image matrix as a sum of 32 stretches, each a column times a row times a strength σ, sorted strongest first.
singular value σ
The strength of one stretch: the bars. Steeply falling bars mean a few stretches carry the picture.
rank-k approximation
The sum of the first k stretches only. It stores k columns, k rows and k strengths.
relative error
Size of what was dropped over size of the whole, both measured as the square root of the sum of squares.
